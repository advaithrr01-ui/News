Record rain kills eight in Chiba as Japan issues its highest warning for the region

The Japan Meteorological Agency recorded 351mm of rain at Chiba station on 13 August, the highest daily total since records began in 1966 and well above the previous mark of 259mm. Eight people were confirmed dead across Chiba City, Ichikawa, Kashiwa, Sakura and Yachiyo, with one person still missing and at least 170 homes damaged. Evacuation orders reached more than 100,000 residents and around 7,000 passengers were stranded overnight at Narita Airport after rail and bus links were cut.

10Science

Webb finds Neptune's inner moons may be debris from a collision triggered by Triton

A Caltech-led team using JWST detected clay-like minerals, which form in the presence of liquid water, on the moons Larissa and Galatea and in Neptune's rings, even though those moons show no obvious water ice. The researchers argue Neptune's original satellite system was destroyed when Triton was captured from elsewhere in the solar system, with debris later coalescing into the small inner moons seen today. Astronomers separately released more than three million new spectra as the SDSS-V survey extended to the Southern Hemisphere.
