08Science

Solar telescope films plasma whirlpools that may explain the corona's heat

Researchers using the NSF Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope on Maui captured Kelvin-Helmholtz instability on the Sun's photosphere as swirling structures only about 20 km across, publishing the results in Nature. The vortices mix magnetised and non-magnetised plasma, speeding the diffusion of magnetic fields into the upper atmosphere. The mechanism is a candidate answer to why the corona reaches a million degrees while the surface stays near 6,000.
