Supreme Court cuts the practice requirement for judicial service entry from three years to one

A 2:1 bench modified its own May 2025 ruling on Friday while dismissing the review petition, leaving one year of active legal practice as the bar for entry-level judicial exams. Selected candidates must still complete judicial academy training and a further year of clerkship. The new regime takes effect from 1 April 2027; candidates for exams notified between 25 May 2025 and 31 March 2027 are exempt from any prior experience requirement.

02Policy

Supreme Court ruling on the meaning of industry narrows labour law cover

The court held that the expansive 1978 Bangalore Water Supply test for what counts as an industry does not automatically carry over to the Industrial Relations Code, 2020. That earlier test looked at the nature of an establishment's activity rather than its profit motive or legal form, and had pulled hospitals and educational institutions into the scope of labour protections. Trade unions warned the split verdict could strip retrenchment safeguards from workers in those sectors.

03Policy

Centre notifies a Rs 62,500 crore mobile phone manufacturing scheme with a carve-out for Indian brands

The five-year scheme, notified on 21 August and running to 2031, splits into one segment for mobile phone manufacturing generally and a second reserved for Indian brands. Indian brands get a flat 5% incentive plus 3% for domestic design and R&D, and up to a further 1.5% for sourcing key components locally. To qualify as Indian a firm must hold its IP and trademark in India, have in-house R&D here and be more than 51% owned by Indian citizens; the government is targeting 60,000 jobs.
