09Science

Researcher proposes folding Australopithecus and Paranthropus into the genus Homo

Writing in the American Journal of Biological Anthropology, Ian Towle of Monash University argues that roughly five million years of hominin fossils should sit in a single genus, because traits once used to separate them, chiefly brain size and tool use, no longer hold. Paranthropus fossils have since 2023 been found alongside 2.6-million-year-old tools in Kenya and Ethiopia. The proposal would resolve the fact that Australopithecus is not a true clade, at the cost of losing useful ecological distinctions.
