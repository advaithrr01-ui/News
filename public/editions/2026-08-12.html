02Science

Europe sees its first total solar eclipse since 1999 this evening

The moon's shadow crosses Siberia, eastern Greenland and western Iceland before reaching northern Spain shortly before sunset, with totality over León at about 18:28 UTC and Valencia at 18:32. Maximum totality lasts 2 minutes 18 seconds. It is the first total eclipse visible from mainland Europe in 27 years and the first from the Iberian Peninsula in over a century.
