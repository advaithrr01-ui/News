02Climate

Scientists trace the Nepal disaster to an ice and rock avalanche that dammed a Himalayan river

Researchers at ICIMOD and Nepal's disaster authority now attribute Wednesday's surge to an ice-rock avalanche that blocked the Lhende Khola before failing and releasing the water downstream, rather than to an earthquake. Satellite imagery reviewed alongside USGS analysis shows a large section of ice breaking away from a glacier above the valley. The finding pushes the event into the category of glacial hazards that are becoming more frequent as high-altitude ice destabilises.

08Science

Astronomers capture the clearest image yet of the companion star orbiting Betelgeuse

Observations with the SPHERE instrument on the European Southern Observatory's Very Large Telescope resolve Betelgeuse B, ending a search that began about a century ago when astronomers first proposed a companion to explain the red supergiant's periodic dimming. The data put the companion at two to three times the mass of the Sun, heavier than earlier estimates. Lead researcher Miguel Montarges of the Observatoire de Paris called it the conclusion of a century-long quest.

08Science

DNA from an 1836 museum specimen confirms a distinct Himalayan pangolin species

Researchers sequenced the type specimen held at the Natural History Museum in London and matched it to modern animals from Nepal, northern India, Bhutan and Myanmar, establishing Manis aurita as a species separate from the Chinese pangolin. The result also renames the population described in 2025 as Manis indoburmanica. Being able to identify the species from scales gives enforcement agencies a way to trace seized shipments back to the region they were poached from.
