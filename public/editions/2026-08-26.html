05Climate

A Manhattan-sized ice island breaks free from Greenland's Petermann Glacier

A 76 sq km slab of the Petermann Glacier's floating ice tongue detached on 4 August, in the glacier's largest loss of floating ice since 2012 and the most significant Arctic calving event since 2020. Sentinel-1 radar imagery showed the ice tongue deteriorating along its centreline the day before it broke away; the new iceberg is up to 150 metres thick. Two further rifts could release ice islands of about 97 and 87 sq km, and researchers will track the drift for effects on Arctic shipping.
