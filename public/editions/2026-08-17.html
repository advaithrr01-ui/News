09Tech & Policy

EU chatbot disclosure rules are now in force but only about ten states are ready to enforce them

Article 50 transparency obligations under the AI Act took effect on 2 August, requiring chatbots to identify themselves, deepfakes to be labelled and AI-generated content to carry machine-readable marks, with penalties up to 15 million euros or 3% of global turnover. The Commission's AI Office is enforcing alongside national authorities, but only around ten member states, including Ireland, Spain, Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands, have advanced public implementation. No major new AI model launch or company announcement surfaced from established outlets in the past 48 hours.
