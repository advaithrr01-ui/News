07Science

The case fatality rate has risen to 46%, with 730 patients in isolation and 1,040 recoveries. 08Science

Caltech brings fibre-optic loss levels to silicon chips at visible wavelengths

Writing in Nature, a Caltech team reported germano-silicate waveguides made with standard semiconductor lithography and surface reflow that cut optical loss up to twentyfold against silicon nitride at visible wavelengths and improved laser coherence more than a hundredfold. The waveguides are laid out in spirals to extend path length in a small footprint. The likely applications are optical clocks, miniature atomic sensors, quantum systems and lower-power data centre interconnects.
