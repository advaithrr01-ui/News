08Science

Hubble spots a growing 10-sided "decagon" encircling Saturn's south pole

Astronomers say Hubble has tracked a giant, evolving 10-sided atmospheric wave around Saturn's south pole, roughly 104,250 miles wide with each side about 10,425 miles long, in findings published in Science Advances. The decagon appears to have emerged only in the past few years, first showing up in 2023 Hubble images, and is still strengthening. It is the first large regular-sided jet pattern seen in Saturn's southern hemisphere, echoing but distinct from the planet's famous northern hexagon discovered in 1988.

09Climate

WMO warns a "very strong" El Nino is set to intensify, raising flood, drought and heat risks into 2027

The World Meteorological Organization says the chance of El Nino persisting through February 2027 is now close to 100%, an unusually unequivocal forecast for the agency. The Nino 3.4 index, a key measure of central Pacific Ocean temperatures, averaged 1.5C above normal from May to July and had climbed to 2.6C above normal by mid-August. The UN agency says the phenomenon raises the likelihood of extreme heat, floods and drought across multiple regions as it peaks around year-end.
